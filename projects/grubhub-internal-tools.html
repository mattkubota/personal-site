Grubhub Internal Tools

2021–2022 Senior Product Designer

Redesigned customer service and operations tools to improve agent efficiency and reduce resolution times through better information architecture and workflow optimization.

Challenge

Grubhub's customer service agents were using outdated tools with complex interfaces, leading to long resolution times and frustrated customers. The fragmented system required agents to navigate multiple applications, causing delays and increasing the likelihood of errors.

Solution

I led the redesign of the internal customer service platform, consolidating multiple tools into a unified interface. The new system prioritized agent workflow efficiency while providing comprehensive customer context and automated resolution suggestions.

Key Features

  • Unified dashboard with complete customer order history
  • Automated issue detection and resolution recommendations
  • One-click actions for common customer requests
  • Real-time collaboration tools for complex cases
  • Performance analytics and agent feedback systems

Impact

The redesigned tools reduced average case resolution time by 40% and improved agent satisfaction scores from 2.8 to 4.2 out of 5. Customer wait times decreased by 35%, and first-call resolution rates increased by 50%.
