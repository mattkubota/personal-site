Curbside SOS

2025 Product Designer

Emergency roadside assistance platform connecting stranded drivers with nearby service providers through real-time location-based matching.

Challenge

Traditional roadside assistance services often leave drivers waiting for hours without clear communication about arrival times or service provider details. The existing solutions lack transparency and real-time updates, creating anxiety during already stressful situations.

Solution

Curbside SOS provides an Uber-like experience for roadside assistance, featuring real-time GPS tracking, transparent pricing, and direct communication with service providers. The platform prioritizes safety and peace of mind through verified providers and emergency features.

Key Features

  • Real-time service provider tracking and ETA updates
  • Transparent, upfront pricing with no hidden fees
  • In-app messaging and calling with service providers
  • Emergency contact integration and safety features
  • Photo documentation for insurance claims

Impact

The platform reduced average response times by 40% and increased customer satisfaction scores to 4.8/5. The transparent pricing model eliminated billing disputes, while real-time tracking reduced customer service calls by 65%.
