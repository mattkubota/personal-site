Vocable AAC

2018—2021 Co-founder & Product Designer

Free, open-source communication app for individuals with speech and language disabilities, designed with accessibility-first principles and community-driven development.

Challenge

Existing AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication) apps were expensive, inaccessible to many families, and often designed without direct input from the disability community. Many solutions cost thousands of dollars and weren't optimized for diverse needs.

Solution

I co-founded Vocable to create a free, accessible AAC app that puts users first. Working directly with speech therapists, caregivers, and users, we designed an interface that adapts to different abilities while maintaining simplicity and effectiveness.

Key Features

  • Switch control and eye-tracking support for motor disabilities
  • Customizable layouts and vocabulary sets
  • High-contrast modes and adjustable text sizes
  • Offline functionality for reliable communication
  • Privacy-focused design with no data collection

Impact

Vocable has been downloaded over 100,000 times and is used in schools, therapy centers, and homes worldwide. The app received Apple's App of the Day recognition and has been featured in accessibility conferences globally. It remains completely free and open-source.
