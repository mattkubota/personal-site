Grubhub Offers

2024 Senior Product Designer

Redesigned the promotional offers experience to increase customer engagement and drive order frequency through personalized deals and gamified rewards.

Challenge

Grubhub's existing offers system had low engagement rates and high abandonment. Users found it difficult to discover relevant deals, and the redemption process was confusing. The lack of personalization led to generic promotions that didn't resonate with individual customer preferences.

Solution

I led the redesign of the offers experience, introducing personalized recommendations based on order history, location, and preferences. The new system featured progressive disclosure, clear value proposition communication, and streamlined redemption flows.

Key Features

  • AI-powered personalized offer recommendations
  • Progressive reward tiers with visual progress indicators
  • One-tap offer activation and automatic application
  • Location-based restaurant promotions
  • Expiration notifications and urgency messaging

Impact

The redesigned offers system increased engagement by 85% and drove a 32% increase in repeat orders. Customer satisfaction with the promotions experience improved from 3.2 to 4.6 out of 5, while offer redemption rates doubled.
