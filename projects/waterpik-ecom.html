Waterpik eCom UX Overhaul

2023 Senior UX Designer

Complete redesign of Waterpik's ecommerce experience, focusing on product discovery and checkout optimization to increase conversion rates.

Challenge

Waterpik's existing ecommerce site had a high cart abandonment rate and users struggled to find the right products for their needs. The checkout process was particularly problematic on mobile devices.

Solution

I redesigned the entire shopping experience with a focus on guided product selection and streamlined checkout:

  • Interactive product finder to help users choose the right device
  • Simplified product pages with clearer benefit messaging
  • One-page checkout optimized for mobile completion
  • Improved search and filtering with visual product comparison

Results

The redesign led to a 32% increase in conversion rate and 45% reduction in cart abandonment. Mobile checkout completion improved by 60%, and customer support tickets related to product selection decreased by 25%.
