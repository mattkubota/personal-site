Grubhub Pickup

2022–2023 Senior Product Designer

Designed and launched Grubhub's pickup experience, reducing wait times and improving restaurant efficiency through better order coordination and customer communication.

Challenge

Pickup orders at Grubhub suffered from poor timing coordination, leading to customers arriving too early or late, and restaurants struggling to manage order readiness. This resulted in long wait times, frustrated customers, and decreased restaurant efficiency.

Solution

I designed a comprehensive pickup experience that included real-time order tracking, smart arrival time predictions, and restaurant-to-customer communication. The solution balanced customer convenience with restaurant operational needs.

Key Features

  • Intelligent pickup time estimation based on restaurant capacity
  • Real-time order status updates and push notifications
  • GPS-triggered arrival notifications to restaurants
  • In-app check-in system for seamless order collection
  • Feedback loop for continuous time prediction improvements

Impact

The new pickup experience reduced average customer wait times by 60% and increased pickup order volume by 45%. Restaurant partners reported improved kitchen workflow efficiency, and customer satisfaction for pickup orders increased to 4.7/5.
